Frequently Asked Questions

Helpful answers about booking, vehicles, airports, weddings and service areas.

How do I book Master Elite Limousine online?

You can book online by completing the form with your contact information, pickup location, drop-off location, date, time, passenger count, luggage details, vehicle preference and any notes about the trip.

What happens after I submit the booking form?

After your request is submitted, we review the details and follow up to confirm availability, timing, service requirements and the best vehicle option for your transportation needs.

Which cities do you serve?

Master Elite Limousine serves Guelph, Milton, Kitchener, Waterloo, Cambridge, Woodstock, London, Windsor, Mississauga, Fergus, Rockwood and Acton.

Do you provide airport transportation?

Yes. We provide private airport car service for departures, arrivals and transfers. You can include your flight number in the booking form so your airport transportation request is easier to review.

What vehicle types can I request?

You can request a Sedan, SUV, Sprinter or Bus depending on your passenger count, luggage needs and occasion. If you are not sure which vehicle is best, choose "Not Sure" in the booking form.

Do you provide wedding limo service?

Yes. We provide wedding transportation for couples, wedding parties, family members and guests. Advance booking is recommended for wedding dates and larger group transportation.

Can I book corporate or executive transportation?

Yes. Corporate car service is available for meetings, hotels, office travel, airport pickups, client transportation, road shows and executive transfers.

How far in advance should I book?

Advance booking is recommended, especially for airport transfers, weddings, special events, corporate travel and group transportation. Earlier requests give more time to confirm the right vehicle and schedule.
