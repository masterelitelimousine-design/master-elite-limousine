Simple Online Booking for Limo, Black Car and Chauffeur Service

Master Elite Limousine makes it easy to request luxury transportation online for airport transfers, corporate travel, concierge and VIP service, private chauffeur bookings, road shows, special events, transfers and wedding transportation. Based in Guelph, Ontario, we serve clients across Milton, Kitchener, Waterloo, Cambridge, Woodstock, London, Windsor, Mississauga, Fergus, Rockwood and Acton. The booking form is designed to collect the information needed to understand your ride and prepare the correct transportation option.

When submitting a booking request, clients can include their service type, pickup location, drop-off location, date, time, passenger count, bags or luggage, vehicle preference, flight number and notes. These details are important because every ride is different. A business traveler may need airport service with luggage, a wedding party may need a Sprinter, and a larger event group may need a bus. Clear information helps Master Elite Limousine provide a smoother response and better service planning.

The FAQ and Book Online page is also a helpful place to understand how our service works. We recommend booking in advance whenever possible, especially for airport transfers, weddings, corporate travel and group transportation. Whether you are requesting a one-way trip, round trip, hourly chauffeur service or special event transportation, Master Elite Limousine is ready to help with premium black car service across Southern Ontario.
